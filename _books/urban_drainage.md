Urban Drainage is a comprehensive reference on modern urban drainage design, planning, and management. It covers the latest approaches to sustainable drainage systems, flooding resilience, and integrated water cycle management for cities. This Routledge volume is a useful resource for environmental engineers, planners, and anyone working on urban water infrastructure.
